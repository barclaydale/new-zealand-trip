Your dates

Late March – early April: what to know

Early autumn. It's shoulder season, so crowds and rates are down from summer while nearly every tour still runs. The weather in the Southern Alps is often at its most settled. Two dates shape the trip: Easter and the end of daylight saving.

Easter 2027

Good Friday 26 Mar · Easter Sunday 28 Mar · Easter Monday 29 Mar. On Good Friday and Easter Sunday most shops and supermarkets close (Queenstown and Wānaka allow Easter Sunday trading), and bars may only serve people who are dining. Cafés and tours mostly run. It's a busy domestic holiday weekend, so book beds and rental cars early.

Clocks go back

Sunday 4 April, 3am → 2am. Daylight saving ends. South Island sunsets move from roughly 7:30–8pm to about 6:30pm, so plan long hikes and sunset spots for the first week.

In season for you

Grape harvest in Marlborough and Central Otago · Bluff oysters (season opens 1 March) · first autumn colour in Arrowtown (peaks mid-to-late April) · royal albatross chicks being fed · Super Rugby season · warm-ish sea in Abel Tasman.

You'll miss

Lupins (Nov–Dec) · whitebait season (Sep–Nov) · Hokitika Wild Foods Festival (usually early to mid March) · Bluff Oyster Festival (May) · peak autumn colours (mid-to-late April). NZ school holidays usually start mid-April, so you should mostly avoid them.

Before you fly

US passport holders need an NZeTA plus the NZ$100 visitor levy, paid together online or in the app. Te Papa now charges internationals NZ$35. A proposed fee for internationals at Milford, Aoraki, Tongariro and Cathedral Cove is planned for summer 2027/28 at the earliest. Bring sandfly repellent for Fiordland and the West Coast.
